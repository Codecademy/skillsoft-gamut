Add the `gamut-migration` agent skill. It covers every `@skillsoft/gamut-codemods` preset: `scope-swap` for the `@codecademy/gamut*` to `@skillsoft/gamut*` move, and `color-roles` for moving color names onto color roles. For each, it explains the warnings, leftovers and comments the codemod leaves, and what to do about them. It replaces the `gamut-scope-swap` skill.
